About Me

I am an Information Technology student with a passion for learning how technology can be used to solve problems and create innovative solutions. I chose Information Technology because I am interested in understanding how computer systems, software applications and digital technologies work.

My career goal is to become a skilled IT professional who can contribute to software development and technology-based projects. I am interested in areas such as programming, application development, databases and information systems.

Throughout my studies, I am developing technical skills in programming, web development, database management, networking and problem-solving. I am also improving my teamwork, communication and critical-thinking skills to prepare for the professional IT environment.

I believe continuous learning is important in the IT industry because technology is always changing. I aim to gain practical experience, expand my knowledge and create solutions that can contribute positively to organisations and communities.
