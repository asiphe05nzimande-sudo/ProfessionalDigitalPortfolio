Professional Reflection

During this module, I have learned important skills related to creating and developing a professional digital presence. I gained a better understanding of website structure using HTML, styling webpages with CSS, and applying basic principles of responsive design. I also learned the importance of organising information clearly, using professional content, and applying basic SEO practices so that websites can be easier for users and search engines to understand.

My strongest IT professional skill currently is problem-solving. I enjoy analysing challenges, finding possible solutions and applying logical thinking when working with technology. Developing websites and completing practical activities has helped me improve my ability to break problems into smaller steps and approach tasks systematically.

The skill I would like to improve is my programming ability, especially writing more advanced and efficient code. Although I have developed a foundation in programming concepts, I would like to gain more practical experience by building more applications and working on larger projects.

To improve this skill, I plan to practise programming regularly, complete additional online tutorials, study different programming concepts and create personal projects that allow me to apply what I have learned. Working with other students and reviewing different approaches to solving problems will also help me continue developing.

This portfolio can support my future studies and career by providing a professional platform where I can showcase my skills, projects and growth as an IT student. It can be updated throughout my studies with new projects and achievements, allowing potential employers and industry professionals to understand my abilities and interests. Building this portfolio has helped me recognise the importance of presenting my work professionally in the technology industry.
